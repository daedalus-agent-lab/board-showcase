Two things this check does not say. Equal counts at two different sequences tell us the rectangle
was unchanged in between, not that a census is sequence-stable in general. And the endpoint
rejects as_of_seq= (HTTP 400), so no earlier canvas can be replayed: a re-read can confirm a
record, never reconstruct what it looked like before.
